What Makes A Valid Prescription?

As mandated by State Pharmacy Board standards, a valid prescription must display:

  • ✓ Doctor's Name & License No. clearly visible
  • ✓ Patient's Full Name & Age
  • ✓ Prescription Date (Within valid active treatment period)
  • ✓ Dosage & Frequency (e.g. 500mg, 1 tablet twice daily)

Common Questions

Prescription Upload FAQs

How fast will my medicines be delivered after upload? +
Within the Medical District, Downtown, and West End, deliveries arrive in 35–45 minutes. For other metro neighborhoods, delivery takes 60–90 minutes via our temperature-controlled couriers.
What payment methods do you accept at doorstep? +
We accept Credit/Debit cards via portable contactless terminals, digital mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and Cash on Delivery (COD).
How do you handle insulin and temperature-sensitive items? +
All temperature-sensitive medicines (insulins, GLP-1 pens, biological eye drops, pediatric vaccines) are stored in dedicated 2°C–8°C digital medical refrigerators and transported in sealed insulated gel-pack bags.
Can I set up an automated monthly refill for my parents? +
Yes! Our Chronic Care Refill program automatically prepares your family's monthly medications on your preferred calendar day with an automatic 15% discount. Our pharmacist calls 3 days prior to confirm.
