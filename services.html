Chronic Care Subscription Program

Designed for diabetic, cardiac, and hypertensive patients. Never worry about running out of daily life-saving pills. We automatically package and deliver your monthly medicine kit 3 days before your previous stock runs out.

Certified Cold-Chain Insulin Handling

Insulin proteins denature and lose efficacy if exposed to ambient heat above 25°C. CityMed stores all biologicals in medical-grade refrigerators with continuous sensor logging and delivers in thermal ice bags.

✦ Delivered in Insulated Thermal Pouches

Diagnostic Lab Tests at Home

Book essential preventative health checks, lipid profiles, HbA1c, thyroid panels, and vitamin D levels. Certified phlebotomists collect blood samples from your home at 7:00 AM, with digital reports delivered on WhatsApp.
