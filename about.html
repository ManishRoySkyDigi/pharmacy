Our Founding Purpose

Setting The Clinical Benchmark in Community Pharmacy

Founded in the Medical District, CityMed was born out of a critical healthcare mission: eliminating the risks of improperly stored medicines, unverified grey-market inventory, and dispensing without qualified pharmacist supervision.

We designed CityMed from the ground up as a patient-first clinical dispensary. Every batch of medication is verified with computerized lot tracking, every refrigeration unit is logged 24/7 with digital alarms, and every consultation is handled by experienced clinical pharmacists.

Safety Systems

The 4 CityMed Quality Invariants

📦

Direct Depot Sourcing

We bypass all third-party brokers and purchase directly from manufacturer facilities with official tax invoices and laboratory certificates of analysis.

❄️

Continuous Cold-Chain

Medical-grade refrigerators with digital telemetry alert our staff if internal temperatures fluctuate beyond the 2°C–8°C threshold for even 3 minutes.

🛡️

Batch & Expiry Guarantee

Every dispensed medication features a minimum 12-month shelf life. All batch IDs are printed on your invoice for complete manufacturer trace.

👨‍⚕️

Double Pharmacist Audit

Prescriptions are checked by two independent qualified pharmacists before dispatch to verify strength, frequency, and drug interaction risks.
